1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The idea fits the track exactly ("school safety on bad days" is a listed Air topic)
and the core AWS path works. The build is NOT podium-ready yet. Four themes cut
across all four audits:

  A. WRONG OR UNTRUSTWORTHY OUTPUT — the rules engine treats fractional/invalid AQI as
     "Severe"; one public GET can push the campus to "Emergency protocol"; timestamps
     are overwritten or misparsed; the audit log can snapshot a different AQI than the
     admin saw and decisions can be silently overwritten. For an "auditable decision
     engine" this is the biggest risk.
  B. THE DEMO WILL VISIBLY BREAK — dark mode hides content, existing alerts don't show
     (campus key mismatch), decision recording fails after 10 alerts, the trend chart
     is an unlabeled flat line of demo data, and Bedrock fails on every call.
  C. "ACT" GOES NOWHERE — alerts are marked "sent" but no one receives them: no
     recipient view and no delivery. This is the main product gap for "Idea & Impact".
  D. NO GUARDRAILS — public write endpoints, no throttling/budget, an unprotected audit
     table, no monitoring, root-account usage, plaintext API keys.
